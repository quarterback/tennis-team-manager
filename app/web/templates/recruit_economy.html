Scholarship Economy — reference

Rosters are built by a scholarship-BUDGET economy, not a flat scholarship count. A program has a budget (by conference tier) and spends it on recruits, who cost a scholarship-equivalency by star. To attract a tier at all a program must also clear that tier's budget floor — that's what makes blue-chips cluster at the blue-bloods.

PRO The pro tier — above blue-chip, portal-only

A small elite cohort of ex-pros sits above the 80 college ceiling — the only players who exceed it. Their attributes are drawn in the {{ econ.pros.attr_lo }}–{{ econ.pros.attr_hi }} band (landing OVR in the mid-80s), shown with a real STR/OVR like anyone else and clearly better on court. They carry the green PRO badge and are free agents out of a synthetic Pros pool — signable only through the portal, never on the recruit board above.
  • {{ econ.pros.per_cycle }} free agents per gender, each portal window (pre-season + fall) — the pool size is tunable live on the Pre-season portal page (0 disables the tier).
  • You sign them by hand to ANY program, ANY division — an unsigned free agent doesn't enter. Scout a pro's full attributes before you commit.
  • Cost {{ '%.1f'|format(econ.pros.cost_lo) }}–{{ '%.1f'|format(econ.pros.cost_hi) }}, indexed to STR — pitched high (near the 33.5 elite budget cap) so one pro eats most of a club's budget: a blue-blood realistically affords one, a mid-major none. Pros spread across clubs instead of stacking.
  • Paid out of the SAME budget as the recruit class — one pool, not a separate pro fund. A pro's cost is deducted for the year, so chasing one means a weaker class: a 28-cost pro on a 33.5 budget leaves ~5 — below the blue-chip floor, so no blue-chips that year. That tradeoff is the whole point of the tier.
  • Signing one bumps the roster's weakest player (a walk-on) — the walk-on tail refills at rollover.

How a roster gets built from the budget
  1. The program draws a budget from its conference-tier band (above), nudged by its own prestige + a per-world jitter.
  2. It signs the best recruit it can afford (cost ≤ remaining budget) and attain (budget ≥ that tier's floor), repeating until the budget is spent.
  3. Remaining roster seats fill with free 1★ (or 2★ at high-prestige D1) walk-on depth.

So a budget-8 D1 program can't just buy a blue-chip (floor 16.5) — it builds a 4★/3★ core. Roster caps: {{ econ.roster_caps['D1'] }} · D2 {{ econ.roster_caps['D2'] }} · D3/D4 {{ econ.roster_caps['D3/D4'] }}.

Prestige is dynamic — it drifts each season by how a program over/under-performs, so budgets move both ways over time. This page reads the live engine constants, so it always matches the current sim.
