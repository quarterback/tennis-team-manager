{# --- Method: the criteria, and the flight check the selector ran ------- #} {# The criteria belong WHERE THE AWARDS ARE. A user looking at a selection they disagree with should be able to see what the selectors weighed without leaving the page for the Guide — in its own view, because it is reference. #}
What the selectors weigh

Selections are made from what happened this season — the match-by-match record — and never from ability ratings. Nothing here reads OVR, talent, class year, recruiting rank or last season's honours, so a great player on a poor team can win Player of the Year, and an unbeaten record against a weak slate does not automatically outrank a strong one against the best in the class.

  • Record and volume — wins, losses and how much the player actually played.
  • Position — the flight it was earned at, and this is structural, not a bonus. 19–7 at #1 singles is a bigger season than 25–1 at #5, because the #1 spent the year playing every other program's best player. All-State is a #1/#2 honour and All-Region reaches #3; below that a player is selected only on extraordinary evidence — a near-perfect record and a win over somebody who played higher up the card. All-District broadens downward, because a district is a smaller pond, but the hierarchy still holds. The Flight check below is the audit.
  • Opposition — the quality of the individual opponents faced (of the opposing PAIR, in doubles), not the opposing team's overall strength.
  • Quality wins — beating other award-calibre players is the fastest way up.
  • Good losses — a competitive loss to an elite opponent costs very little; a loss to a weak one costs plenty.
  • Head-to-head — decides between candidates whose résumés are otherwise level.
  • Postseason — Sectionals through State count for more. There is no individual state tournament in Jefferson; this is the team postseason the player played.

Doubles honours go to pairings, not to individual doubles players. A doubles selection is two players who actually competed together, judged on the matches they played as a team against other teams — so a player who partnered around is a separate candidate with each partner, and the record beside a pairing is the partnership's. Which category an athlete is considered in follows where they actually played, so nobody holds both a singles slot and a doubles slot on the same team.

The three honours are three different geographies, and only two of them are per classification. All-State is the classification. A district is (classification, name) — the association reuses its geographic names at every level — so All-District is too. All-Region is neither: a region team is drawn from every program in that region whatever its enrollment, which is how it works in real life and is why there is no {{ view.group }} All-Region team, only a Gold Valley one. Selected per classification it honoured roughly a thousand players a season on an association of ~300 programs — every school placed somebody, and it was an All-District team with a different heading.

Every All-State, All-Region and All-District team is {{ view.team_singles }} singles + {{ view.team_doubles }} doubles teams — {{ view.team_singles + 2 * view.team_doubles }} athletes. 7A crowns a Fourth Team; every other classification stops at Third. Honorable Mention is a threshold, not a team — there is no slot count, so a deep classification honours more and a thin one fewer, and a good season can miss it entirely. At most two Honorable Mention selections per school (a pairing is one); the numbered teams have no such cap. A team can only be as large as the candidates who cleared the bar, so a thin region occasionally crowns one pairing fewer rather than honouring one that did not earn it.
