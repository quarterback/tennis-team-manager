How junior ranking points work

This is the junior accomplishment ledger — modelled on the USTA / ITF junior points system — and it is deliberately separate from the recruiting board. Where the board ranks on a consensus of visible ability + scouts' ceiling read, this ranks purely on what a recruit earned on the junior circuit.

  1. Points by round & level. Each event awards points for the round reached, scaled by its level (Major → Premier → National → Development → State, i.e. USTA Levels 1–5). A Major title is worth 3000; a State quarterfinal, 105.
  2. Best six count. Only a player's best six results (plus their best six ranked-win bonuses) are summed — so grinding weak events has limits, but deep runs at strong events pay.
  3. Ranked-win bonus. Beating a higher-ranked junior adds points (a Top-10 win is worth +225), USTA-style.
  4. STR is the rating, not the ranking. The STR column is the results-based strength rating (the WTN analogue). A player ranked low on points but high on STR is a gem who skipped events; the reverse is a grinder. The two diverging is the signal.
