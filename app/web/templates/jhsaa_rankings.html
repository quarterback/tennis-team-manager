TOSS — 40% strength of schedule, 40% flight-weighted results, 20% game share. It is computed over the whole association's regular season, not one classification, and is archived with the season: at-large bids and state seeds were drawn from exactly these numbers, so the ranking and the bracket cannot disagree.

ATR — the Average Team Rating, half TOSS and half win percentage, archived the same way. It is the only figure here that is not pure TOSS, and it exists for one job: ranking the Conference pool, the last recovery round. TOSS measures who you played, so a middling team in a brutal district is propped up by the company it keeps while a twenty-win season against an ordinary schedule rates below it — the right trade for seeding a draw, the wrong one for the last seat in the field. Seasons archived before ATR existed show a dash.

The SHOWCASE columns compare each team's mid-season 1S/4D Match Showcases against its 5S/2D regular season — the format State is decided in against the format the regular season is played in. Fmt Shift is showcase weighted win share minus regular-season weighted win share: positive means a team plays UP once the card drops to one singles line and four doubles. Dbl Shift is the same comparison for how much of a team's wins come from doubles specifically — positive means doubles is carrying more of the win under 1S/4D than it did all season. SC Volatility is how much a team's result swings dual to dual within its showcases; a smaller card means one line flipping moves a bigger share of the outcome. None of this is TOSS or ATR, none of it is archived toward a seed, and none of it means anything on a thin sample — hover any of these cells for the dual count (SC n) it was built on, and a team with n=1 has played one dual, not established a trend.
