The {{ proj.field }}-team field if the tournament were seeded today. Selection: projected conference leaders take the {{ proj.aq|length }} automatic bids (AQ); the remaining {{ cut }} at-large spots (AL) go to the best of the rest by Committee Seed Score. Seeding: the whole field is then ranked by that same score — a committee-style blend of Power Index (base strength, 45%), team ranking points (résumé, 30%), a tiered championship bonus for automatic qualifiers (15%, larger for power-conference champs), and recent form (10%). AQ and AL interleave, so a strong at-large can out-seed a weak champion while a power-conference title still lifts a comparable team above it. How a team qualified never changes its seed. Each men's / women's D1–D4 season is its own separate tournament.
