# JHSAA 2094 Annual Report

This report covers both 2094 seasons, girls in spring and boys in fall, and reads them against 2093 and the years before. It draws on the association's research exports for 2087 through 2094. Eleven programs per gender changed class this year, the ordinary yearly reassignment rather than a full realignment cycle, and none of the season's main storylines trace back to it.

## The year in brief

Canal View won a state title for the first time in its history. The program moved into Group 1 last year, lost a semifinal as the 2 seed, and this year won it as the 9 seed, beating Minidoka, Eden, Pendleton, Irvington and Terrace Park along the way. It is the program's first title after 21 trips to state and four second place finishes in 9A.

Westfield Friends won 5A for a third straight year, beating Condotti Vanguard Academy 4-1 in the final and finishing 32-0. Condotti had gone 30-2 in its second year down from 7A. Condotti's girls team had the opposite result: it went 42-2 and won 5A, beating Romero-Finniski's girls 4-1 in the final. The two rivals split their first two years at the lower level almost evenly: Romero-Finniski's girls won the title in 2093, Condotti's girls won it in 2094.

Pacific Friends girls went 40-1 and won 6A for the third year running, then reached the Tournament of Champions final. Seawall girls went 33-0 and won both 8A and the Tournament of Champions.

Three programs won a first state title: Ashbrook boys and girls, both in Group 2, and Singleton and Sacred Heart girls, in Group 3 and 7A. Coastal Christian swept 1A in both genders.

No program in either gender collapsed from a winning season to a losing one, the second time that has happened since these reports began.

## Stories of the year

### Canal View's first title, from the 9 seed

Twenty one trips to state, four seasons finishing second in 9A between 2089 and 2092, a move to Group 1 last year that ended in a semifinal loss as the 2 seed. This year Canal View was seeded 9th and won it anyway, needing five rounds after a first round bye: 8-1 over Minidoka, 5-4 over Eden, 7-2 over Pendleton, 7-2 over Irvington and 6-3 over Terrace Park.

The girls team, seeded 2nd, lost its semifinal 5-4 to Boyle Heights, one round short of giving the school both titles in its first year of eligibility for either.

### Westfield Friends and the rivals who followed them down

Condotti Vanguard Academy and Romero-Finniski moved from 7A to 5A in the 2093 realignment, ending 67 years of playing above their enrollment. In year one, Westfield Friends beat Condotti in the boys final and Romero-Finniski's girls beat Condotti's girls to reach the final, which the Romero girls lost. In year two, Westfield beat Condotti in the boys final again, and this time Condotti's girls beat Romero-Finniski's girls in the final.

Between them, the two rivals have now played in three of the four 5A finals since the move and won one. Westfield Friends has been in every boys final and won both.

### Ashbrook, twice

Ashbrook won Group 2 in both genders, each time by winning the deciding tiebreakers after a 3-3 dual: over Mirage Crossing for the boys and Nyssa for the girls. Neither program had won a state title before. The boys reached the final as the 4 seed and the girls as the 4 seed as well.

### Singleton and Sacred Heart

A year ago Singleton girls went 8-18. This year they went 29-4, won Group 3 as the top seed, and beat Nicodemus 5-0 in the final without dropping a set in the final round. It is the program's first title in either gender.

Sacred Heart girls won 7A from the 8 seed, beating Plainfield 5-4 in the final. It is also a first title for the program.

### Porterfield, twice over

Porterfield boys went 40-1 and reached the 3A final, the best season in program history, and lost 3-2 to Cherry Hill North. The girls team went 30-1 and won 3A, the program's first title since 2065, beating Texas Beach 3-2 in the final. Between them Porterfield produced the four highest win totals of any player in the association: Easton Moss and Lorenzo Erazo at 40-1 each, Lautaro Kühn at 39-1 and Noah Nam at 38-2.

### Perfect and nearly perfect seasons

Seawall girls (33-0) and Westfield Friends boys (32-0) both went unbeaten and both won their Tournament of Champions. Pacific Friends girls went 40-1, and the one loss was a 4-3 Tournament of Champions final against Seawall.

## Players

### Players of the year

Ten of the twelve girls winners were seniors or juniors. Among the boys, two were freshmen: Landry Kelly of Truman, the 4A winner at 9th grade, and none at Group 3, though Jose Clendenin of Pine Eagle won Group 2 as a sophomore.

Condotti Vanguard Academy's Mylah Cox was 5A player of the year at 35-5, the individual centerpiece of the program's title. Her teammate Cari Clarkson, a freshman, led all girls with 42 wins.

### No. 1 singles champions

Nine of the twelve boys singles champions were also their class's player of the year. Jasiah Smith of Cherry Hill North won 3A as the 6 seed. Jayden Campos of Punta del Águila won 1A as the 4 seed. Among the girls, Jaylah Flippo, a Singleton freshman, won Group 3 as the top seed the same week her team won the state title. Sophia Canfield of La Grande won Group 1 from the 6 seed.

### Unbeaten in 20 or more matches

Lorenzo Erazo of Porterfield went 38-0 at No. 2 doubles. Hal Mathey of Texas Beach went 31-0 at No. 1 singles and finished second for 3A player of the year on the strength of it. Tiare Buatava and Teagan Smith, both freshmen at Pacific Friends, went 33-0 apiece in doubles.

## What the numbers say

### Close duals

About 25 percent of varsity duals were decided by one point, and 34 percent of state duals, close to the six year norm. Fifty four duals ended level and went to the three tiebreakers, including both Group 2 finals.

### Teams that outran or trailed their flight record

The same check as in past reports: which teams won more or fewer duals than the flights they actually won would predict, and are therefore candidates to move back toward that level next year.

**Likely to slip in 2095:**
- Boys: Fallon (8A, 27-4, 10-1 in one point duals), Seawall (8A, 27-5, 9-0), Roscoe Village (6A, 22-6, 8-0)
- Girls: Wales City (6A, 19-14, 11-3 in one point duals), Kettle Ridge (5A, 19-15, 10-2), Odellville (1A, 14-17, 11-4)

**Likely to climb:**
- Boys: Quarry Workers (7A, 10-17, 2-6 in one point duals), Quartzburg (2A, 17-14, 1-8), Hollywood (8A, 1-23, 0-4)
- Girls: Severn (7A, 19-11, 0-5 in one point duals), Fort Carden (8A, 6-16, 1-9), Cedar (Group 1, 15-16, 1-6)

### Droughts and firsts

Pine Barrens boys, New Casper boys and Ford boys each reached a final four for the first time in program history. Ford boys reached the final itself for the first time, as did Glenwood girls and Coastal Christian boys. Puerto Alma boys ended a 35 year wait for a semifinal and Bidwell boys a 33 year wait for a final.

## Program arcs

### Biggest climbs

- **Boys:** Larchmont Ridge (8A), 6-17 to 35-1 and the title; Delbarton (8A), 2-25 to 17-16; Kernwood County (7A), 10-17 to 24-7; Tamarack Harbor (9A), 0-21 to 8-16, the program's first winning stretch since its 2067 revival was still out of reach but a real step; Vermillion (Group 1), 21-13 to 25-2.
- **Girls:** Singleton (Group 3), 8-18 to 29-4 and the title; Palisade Prep (3A), 8-16 to 26-5; Delbarton (8A), 4-22 to 21-14; Halbrook (9A), 10-17 to 24-8; Archbishop Gregory (9A), 1-24 to 11-16.

### Biggest falls

- **Boys:** Trois Ilets (9A), 28-7 to 9-17; La Grande (Group 1), 24-3 to 15-15; Rogue Valley (9A), 24-5 to 15-16; Natchez (Group 2), 22-5 to 11-13.
- **Girls:** Grayston (9A), 20-6 to 9-18; Rogue Valley (9A), 23-5 to 13-17; Morne Rouge (7A), 15-15 to 3-22; Jamaica (1A), 25-2 to 20-14.

Rogue Valley fell in both genders. Its 2091 transfer cohort has graduated out, and the program is back near where it was before the arrivals.

## Notes on the data

Programs are matched on program id throughout. The 23 school renames applied ahead of this season (Gruesome Ridge to Arthur Ashe and 22 others) carried through cleanly and are reflected under the new names. Records, honors and titles are as the exports state them. No player ratings are used anywhere in this report. A collapse means winning at least 70 percent of duals one year and under 30 percent the next; none occurred in either gender this year.
