## Conventions

- Headlines: mascot + violent verb + mascot, alliterative or punning ("Lions lambaste
  Lancers", "All's well with Elwell"). Town nicknames ("Tongie", "O-North"). A
  subhead carries the score.
- Leads: road results open score-first or player-first behind a dateline. Staffed
  games use a one-line teaser ("Now it's an annual thing.") with the score in the
  second or third paragraph.
- Order: lede, score/site/day, coach quote, key sequence, quote, stat lines, next
  game with time, then box.
- Records in parentheses at first mascot mention: "LHS (4-1 overall, 3-0 Sunflower
  League)". Also "whose team fell to 0-4".
- Scores winner first, set off by commas: "toppling Shawnee Mission West, 7-4, at Ice
  Field on Thursday".
- Quotes almost always the home coach, "said". Staff reports are built from the
  coach's phone call ("were cited for superb play").
- Vocabulary: twinbill, nightcap, blank, flatten, torch, trouncing, scamper, romp,
  carded, double winner, state berth, "city" for the two local schools.
- Agate: league or state results trimmed to the top six or eight, plus local
  finishers at any place.
- Texas metro: class prefixed to the school ("Class 3A Gainesville"), districts as
  "12-5A", city-prefixed names (FW Dunbar, Arl. Lamar), the paper's own polls by
  class, state pairings listed for every class.

## Tennis

- A dual or quad is a 150-word staff report: the top player's day, pro-set scores
  ("8-0, 8-0 and 8-1"), team scores, coach quotes, next dual.
- Tournament stories attach seed and school to every name: "Topeka High's No. 16
  seed, ...".
- Staffed matches narrate by games and points: "Leading ..., 5-4, and serving",
  "30-all", "broke ...'s serve".
- Agate runs round by round with "def.", hyphenated pairs, school after the comma.
- Season record comes late: "finished the year at 23-7".
- Best single model: `ljw_2002-05-14_alls_well_with` (regional story plus full draw).
