**Recurring conventions**
- Weeklies are unsigned or "From River City High School"; no dateline. The daily uses "By NAME / Times-News writer" and a dateline when on the road (BOISE -).
- Leads: one long summary sentence — how + winner + score + occasion + site ("A couple of close decisions fell in Crookston's favor Tuesday as the Pirates edged..."). 1990s daily leads delay the score to graf 2-3.
- Order: lede, record, winners by position, coach quote, next match, agate. Weekly football/basketball is strict chronology, quotes last or absent.
- Quotes: 0-2 in weeklies, coach only; 1980s verbs vary (explained, noted, acknowledged, stated, pointed out). By 1997 the daily uses "said" only and quotes players.
- Headlines: Up-Style Caps, verb-driven (Tip, Edge, Whip, Shades, Romp, thrash), often a score kicker with ellipsis ("Crookston 4, TRF 3 . . .").
- Vocabulary: netters, net tourney, dual meet, season mark, returned to the win column, knotted the score, gift tosses, twinbill.
- Records after every result ("upped its season mark to 4-1"); class year on first reference; opponents as "NAME of SCHOOL".

**Tennis specifically**
- Told by position: "at No. 1 singles, where junior Bret Haugen defeated Keith Johnson 7-6, 6-0"; verbs outlasted, blanked, cruised, breezed, double bagel.
- Team score first, then who supplied the points; the deciding match gets the detail. Forfeits and darkness are reported flatly.
- Agate block is standard: "TRF 6, Mahnomen-Waubun 1 / Singles - 1. Bret Haugen (TRF) def. ..."; pairs hyphenated; Exhibition (JV) pro sets listed.
- Formats differ and shape the prose: MN 4S+3D; MD/WV pro sets; ID five events incl. mixed, team points, "challenge match" for second; SoCal round-robin sets (10-8 team scores, 44-1 individual records).
- Previews are templated: school subhead, coach, returners with records, projected lineup, one quote.
