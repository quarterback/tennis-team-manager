**Recurring conventions.**
- Leads: score/result-first with day and site for gamers; "expectation" leads ("It came as no surprise…", "This time it wasn't easy."); one-line teaser leads on features. Puns welcome (net, serve).
- Order: result → consequence (record, seed, next opponent) → how it was decided → one or two coach quotes → remaining results → schedule line ("Play continues today at 9 a.m.").
- Quotes: few (0-3 in gamers), nearly always the coach, attribution "said" after the name with title ("Reidsville coach Bill Howell said"). Features stuff age, height, class, rank, GPA into attribution clauses. Roundups often have none.
- Scores: "74-61 victory", record in parentheses after the nickname "(21-1)", conference then overall "7-0 record (14-2 overall)". Class labels everywhere (District 8-3A, Triad 3-A, Region 7-AAAA).
- Headlines: all caps, present tense, short verb: NIPS, TRIPS, ROLLS OVER, OUSTS, TAKES, CAPTURES.
- Vocabulary: netters, tourney, crown, berth, semis, downed, topped, ousted, blanked, edged, stunned, breezed, paced, "Lady" teams, nickname as subject (the Whirlies, the Rams), town-prefixed schools (Eden Morehead, Pensacola-Washington).
- Notebooks/columns: one lead item, then items under capitalised tags ("RAIDER INJURED:", "NOTES:").

**Tennis specifically.**
- Dual gamers narrate the running team score as flights finish ("to make it 4-1… trim the lead to 4-3") and name the clinching flight. Positions are "No. 1 singles", "at No. 4"; pairs hyphenated or "the doubles team of X and Y".
- Lineup paragraphs: position, class year, record, a phrase of playing style.
- Format is explained inline ("under high school rules…"); pro-set doubles (10-6, 8-0), third-set tiebreakers, matches halted once clinched.
- Florida state/district events are flighted with team points; NC has separate individual and dual-team playoffs; Georgia is 3 singles + 2 doubles.
- Roundups give winners' names, usually not set scores; agate: "No. 1-Thomas (CP) d. Berger 6-4, 6-7, 7-6".
- Themes: seeds and upsets, junior rankings, private-vs-public, conduct penalties, rain, travel.
