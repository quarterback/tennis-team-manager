**Recurring conventions.**
- Leads: one sentence carrying winner, score, league and day ("...in a Whitepine League volleyball match Friday night"). Features use a delayed or anecdotal lead, then a one-line turn paragraph ("But the first quarter was enough.").
- Order: lead → what it means (title, berth, record) → one coach quote → stat leaders → next opponent → JV score → agate. Roundups repeat that as capsules under a bold score line.
- Quotes: almost always the coach, one or two, attributed "said"; players quoted only in features and staffed finals. Coaches say "the kids".
- Records in parentheses, overall then league: "(7-0, 5-0)". 1990 copy sets them off with commas ("the Panthers, 24-2,").
- Headlines: 1990–94 ALL CAPS, short, punning on nickname or surname ("EAGLES PIN BANTAMS", "STRONG PLAY NETS TROY WIN"); by 1997 sentence case with a semicolon deck.
- Vocabulary: netters, harriers, cage, tourney, crown, berth, loser-out, play host to, went the distance, paced, edged, topped, eked out, bounced, turn out for. Schools by initials or nickname on second reference (LHS, CVHS, U-Hi, Gar-Pal, T-O).
- Unstaffed games admit gaps ("Falls Christian scoring was unavailable").

**Tennis specifically.**
- A dual is three sentences plus agate: lead with team score and the standout position, one coach quote, next match, then results by category.
- Agate: "Marci Reed, CV, def. Maureen Slichter 6-4, 6-3" — winner gets the school abbreviation; doubles pairs hyphenated; positions numbered.
- Rural Idaho duals are co-ed, 12 points including mixed doubles, can tie 6-6; lower positions play one pro set (10-4). Washington separates boys and girls with no mixed; Wyoming plays 2 singles + 3 doubles in the fall with flighted state titles.
- Tournament copy is seed-driven: "never dropped a game", bracket path as a string of scores, who qualifies for "State", court sites and start times.
- Hometown framing even in defeat: every entry's state W-L is listed, even 0-2.
- Staffed, quote-rich tennis gamers appear only in the 2001–02 Cheyenne pieces.
