RENAMING IS SAFE AND IS THE DESIGNED PATH — but key it on the right thing:

  * A rename goes in `scripts/import_jhsaa.py` -> RENAMES, keyed on the
    SOURCE key (the last column here), never on the display name. The source
    is the school's permanent identity: it seeds the RNG that builds its
    twelve players and the pids on their records. Rename through RENAMES and
    the roster, the history and the archived awards all follow the school.
    Rewrite the name in the data file directly and the program gets twelve
    strangers and its honours point at nobody.
  * If a school ALREADY has a RENAMES entry, rewrite that entry's target in
    place. Never chain A -> B -> C.
  * A DISPLAY NAME MUST BE UNIQUE across the whole association — it is the
    archive identity. Two schools sharing one name silently merge into one
    archive slot and nothing errors.
  * NO INSTITUTIONAL SUFFIX: a trailing 'High School' / 'HS' / 'School' is
    stripped at emit. Write names in full if you like; they emit bare.
  * MASCOTS / COLORS / PRIVATE_SCHOOLS and data/jhsaa/archetypes.json all key
    on the DISPLAY name, so those keys move with a rename.
  * NEVER RENAME A REAL PERSON'S SCHOOL. The person-named pool mixes invented
    names with genuine ones — every president, plus Thurgood Marshall, Octavia
    Butler, James Baldwin, Gwendolyn Brooks, Mae Jemison, Bayard Rustin, John
    Lewis, Ella Baker, Katherine Johnson, Rita Moreno, George Washington
    Carver and others. 'Looks like a person' is not the test.
