## Longitude Anchors (West to East)

- **Westernmost**: Curry County, OR (≈124.5°W) — *Selquah, Sebastian Cape*
- **Easternmost**: Oneida County, ID / Rich County, UT (≈111.2°W) — *Bear River Country*
- **Typical center**: ≈120–122°W

## Key Boundaries

- **North**: Payette/Owyhee boundary (≈44.1°N)
- **South**: Plumas/Nevada/Sierra counties (≈39.2°N)  
- **West**: Curry County OR coast (≈124.5°W)  
- **East**: Rich County UT / Oneida County ID (≈111.2°W)

## Quick Visualization Tip

Think of Jefferson as an irregular rectangle spanning:
- **~500 miles west to east** (124.5°W to 111.2°W)
- **~550 miles north to south** (44.1°N to 39.2°N)
- Occupying parts of OR, CA, ID, NV, UT, WY

The **expanded eastern territory** (FSAC merge regions, 2041+) adds Idaho panhandle, Utah's Cache/Rich, and Wyoming's Lincoln/Uinta counties — already included above.
