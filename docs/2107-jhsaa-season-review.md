# The 2033 (2107) JHSAA Tennis Season

*Internal annual review*

This was the first season of the Circuit round, which replaced the second half of the old recovery road in nine classes. It was also the first season with Condotti Vanguard Academy and Romero-Finniski in 11B. Zonal champions kept winning State titles. The Circuit round changed who gets to play in them.

## Executive findings

**The Circuit round ran in nine classes: 4A, 3A, 2A, 1A, Groups 1 to 3, 10B and 11B.** In each, 32 eligible teams were grouped by location into 8 Circuits of 4. Super Regionals served as the Circuit semifinals, 16 duals a class, and the Circuit final took the place of Semi-State. The 144 Circuit champions (72 each for boys and girls) advanced to State. In 1A to 4A and Group 3 the State field grew from 16 teams to 24 (1A, Group 3) or 32 (2A to 4A).

**Zonal champions still won the titles.** Of the 18 State champions in the nine Circuit classes, 17 had won their Zonal. No Circuit champion reached a State final. Four reached a semifinal: Grizzly Gulch (4A boys), Sotomayor (4A girls), and Jamaica and Windward (1A girls).

**Dufur won the Group 3 boys title the long way.** It lost its Regional, won a Super Regional, lost the Circuit final 3-2 to Klickitat, won the Divisional 4-1, and then won four State duals as the 5 seed, finishing 4-1 over Northrup Tech. It is the first champion to come through the Divisional after losing a Circuit final.

**Chaparral's boys won 9A and the Tournament of Champions.** They went 39-2 and beat Plainfield 4-1 in the TOC final, with freshman Damon Soto and sophomore Brayden Beal each winning 37 of 40 flights. Mater Dei's girls went 43-0, won 10B 5-4 over Jesuit, and then won the TOC 3-2 over California Canyons.

**All four 9A boys semifinalists were boosted programs for the third straight year:** Chaparral, Forest Park, L.B. Johnson and Seawall. Pacific Gate, left alone, went 28-2 as the 2 seed under Nolan Martinez and lost in the Round of 16.

**The large city programs held their ground in year two.** All 22 made State again. They went from winning 87 percent (boys) and 90 percent (girls) of their matches to 82 and 84 percent. Four reached a final: Valderra and Belyakov Vo-Tech among the boys, Roosevelt and Narragansett among the girls. None won a title.

**Five programs won a first state title:** California Beach (1A boys), Dufur (Group 3 boys), Georgia Mills (11B boys), Hayes (7A boys) and Natchitoches (9A girls).

**Condotti Vanguard Academy and Romero-Finniski moved from 10B to 11B in both genders.** Condotti's boys and girls both reached the 11B semifinals.

**No program fell from a winning season to under 30 percent in either gender.**

**The 2107 transfer class landed.** 1,702 of the 1,856 players in the file were on 2107 rosters and 1,701 were at the school named. The other 154 were on no 2107 roster. Nobody was sent to Trois Ilets.

## The first Circuit round

**What ran.** 32 teams in each of the nine classes went into 8 Circuits of 4, built by sorting the teams by area, county and city and dealing them 4 at a time. Each Circuit played two Super Regional duals and a Circuit final. The 8 Circuit champions joined the 8 Zonal champions in the State field.

**Geography.** The median Circuit spans 140 miles between its two farthest teams, and 56 of the 144 Circuits sit inside a single named area. The widest runs 649 miles, the 11B girls Circuit that joins Ashbury Metro and Bear River Country, won by Romero-Finniski. Group 1 boys, Group 3 boys and Group 3 girls each had a Circuit joining Bear River Country with Blue Mountain Country, 473 to 535 miles apart.

**Who made State.** In these nine classes the average State field grew from 22.7 teams to 29.8. Considering eight equal geographic sectors in each class, 94 percent of class seasons now have a State team from every sector, up from 72 percent in 2106. The named areas with a State team rose from 8.3 to 8.9 of the 10.9 the average class has. In the five classes that were 16 team fields, the areas with a State team went from 4.5 to 7.0 in 1A, 7.5 to 9.0 in 2A, 10.0 to 11.5 in 3A and 8.5 to 11.0 in 4A. Group 3 held at 7. Part of that is the larger field itself.

**Deepest runs.** Four Circuit champions reached a State semifinal: Grizzly Gulch's boys (the 11 seed) and Sotomayor's girls (the 16 seed) in 4A, and Jamaica's girls (the 13 seed) and Windward's girls (the 4 seed, 29-7) in 1A. Windward lost the 1A semifinal 4-1 to Mt Jacqueline, the champion.

## The Tournament of Champions

**Boys.** Forest Park, the 9A runner up, beat Belyakov Vo-Tech 3-2 and Mercy Academy Valley, the 11B runner up, beat Hazel Country Day 4-1 in the qualifier. Mercy Academy Valley then lost 3-2 to Chaparral in round one. Forest Park beat Toussaint 4-1 and Chaminade 3-2 and reached the semifinal, where Plainfield beat it 5-0. Chaparral beat Meridian 3-2, Texas Beach 5-0 and Plainfield 4-1 in the final.

**Girls.** Gottschalk-Herman beat Jesuit 3-2 and Narragansett beat Lone Pine 3-2 in the qualifier, and both lost in round one: Gottschalk-Herman 4-1 to Mater Dei and Narragansett 3-2 to California Canyons. Mater Dei beat Perry Green 3-2, Mt Jacqueline 4-1 and California Canyons 3-2. California Canyons reached the final on 3-2 wins over Narragansett, Monongahela and Canal View.

**Qualifier record since it began.** Of 20 qualifier winners across five seasons, two won the Tournament of Champions (Pacific Gate girls in 2104, Jesuit boys in 2106), five more reached a semifinal or final, and 13 were out within two rounds.

## Pre high school development, third season

There were 514 seventh graders and 927 eighth graders, 1,441 in all, up from 1,342. 69 percent of the seventh graders and 73 percent of the eighth graders appeared in a varsity dual, and they won 54 and 49 percent of their flights. 368 of the 12,906 seniors had played early, the first graduating group to have done so.

The boost recorded for early players has a median of 15.5 percent of base ceiling. The bottom tenth got 5.3 percent or less and the top tenth got 39.1 percent or more. 27.7 percent got 25 percent or more, 4.0 percent got 50 percent or more, and 28.3 percent got under 10 percent. 55 percent of recorded players have two early seasons. Compared with classmates in the same program and grade, early players in grades 10 to 12 sit about 0.63 standard deviations ahead on ability, level with 0.65 a year ago.

## Class changes

Condotti Vanguard Academy and Romero-Finniski moved from 10B to 11B in both genders. Romero-Finniski's boys went from 3-15 to 4-10 and its girls from 7-11 to 15-6, reaching the Round of 16. Georgia Mills (boys) and Cassius (girls) won the two 11B titles.

## Alternate 2107

Each entry changes one named result. Everything before it is as played.

**Fork: Barley Gap closes one flight in the 1A boys final.** California Beach won 3-2. Flip one flight and Barley Gap, 28-1 and the top seed, wins the title, and California Beach's first title waits.

**Fork: Klickitat loses the Group 3 boys Circuit final.** Dufur lost it 3-2. Flip one flight and Dufur is a Circuit champion, goes into State as a Circuit champion without ever playing the Divisional, and the route that produced the title never exists.

**Fork: Mercy Academy Valley closes one flight against Chaparral.** Chaparral won the TOC first round 3-2. Flip one flight and the 11B runner up beats the 9A champion in round one, and Chaparral's title run ends there.

**Fork: Monongahela closes the tiebreaker in the Group 2 boys final.** Toussaint won on tiebreakers after 3-3. Flip it and Monongahela, 31-2 and the top seed, wins a third Group 2 title.
