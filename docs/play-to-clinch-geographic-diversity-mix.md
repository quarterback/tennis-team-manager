# Play to Clinch — Geographic Diversity Mix

Reference configuration for rebuilding the Tennis Manager world nationality mix.

## Target continent ranges

| Region | Restart range | Final reference |
|---|---:|---:|
| Americas | 42–45% | ~43.5% |
| Europe | 19–22% | ~20.8% |
| Asia | 15–17% | ~16.1% |
| Africa | 14–16% | ~15.1% |
| Oceania | 4–5% | ~4.2% |
| Zaryanovia | ~0.1% | ~0.1% |

**US share:** keep at roughly **30% of the whole world**.

The goal is geographic diversity without making the continents artificially equal. Europe was intentionally reduced from roughly 26.6% to about 20.8%.

## Useful subregional anchors

These do not need to match exactly, but they reproduce the intended geographic texture well.

### Africa
- Sub-Saharan Africa: ~5.4%
- Africa (Cricket Nations): ~3.4%
- North Africa (Maghreb & Egypt): ~6.2%

### Asia
- East Asia: ~3.9%
- South Asia (cricket pipeline): ~1.4%
- Philippines: ~1.4%
- Malaysia: ~1.3%
- Indonesia: ~1.2%
- South Korea: ~0.8%
- China: ~1.3%
- Japan: ~1.8%
- Kazakhstan: ~0.7%

### Oceania
- Australia / New Zealand: ~3.5%
- Pacific Islands: ~0.7%

## Design intent

A good rebuilt world should land approximately at:

**Americas 43–44 / Europe 20–21 / Asia 16 / Africa 15 / Oceania 4–5**

This preserves meaningful historical/geographic asymmetry while preventing the player pool from collapsing into an overwhelmingly Europe + Americas talent universe.
