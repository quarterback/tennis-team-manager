# JHSAA 2092 Annual Report

This report covers both 2092 seasons, girls in spring and boys in fall, and reads them against 2091 and the years before. It is built from the association's research exports for 2088 through 2092.

The class map did not change. Between seasons the only roster moves were the intake transfers into Canal View, Rogue Valley and Kernwood County, and the players those programs sent down a class. The reserve cohorts built last winter were not installed.

## The year in brief

Rogue Valley boys won their first state title, beating Canal View 5-4 in the 9A final. They then won the Tournament of Champions. Two years ago Rogue Valley was going out in the round of 32 for the fourth straight season.

Canal View boys went 38-1. It is the fourth straight year Canal View has lost exactly one dual, and all four losses were state finals. Across those four seasons the program is 125-4.

Westfield Friends and Barley Gap swept both genders in their classes for the second year running. Romero-Finniski won 7A in both genders and the girls Tournament of Champions.

Four boys programs won a first state title: Rogue Valley, Cheney, Cedar and Kuusamo. Frontier girls won their first since 2029.

No program in either gender fell from winning 70 percent of its duals to under 30 percent, the first year without one since these reports began in 2089.

Last year's report named teams whose records were likely to slip or climb. The slippers slipped and the climbers climbed, in both genders.

## Stories of the year

### Canal View, 125-4

Canal View boys have gone 28-1, 30-1, 29-1 and 38-1. The four losses came in four 9A finals: 5-4 to Southridge Christian, 7-2 to Jesuit, 5-4 to Jesuit, and 5-4 to Rogue Valley. Canal View has not lost a regular season, league or early round state dual since 2088.

This was the strongest of the four teams. Justin Ellsworth, who arrived from Mt Jacqueline, was 9A player of the year at 30-8 from No. 1 singles. Torrance Schlecht went 35-4 at No. 2. Tyler Thompson, the 3A player of the year as a freshman a year ago, went 29-1 at No. 1 doubles. Canal View beat L.B. Johnson 7-2 in the semifinal and went into the final as the top seed.

Canal View brings back 47 percent of its flight wins. Ellsworth graduates.

### Rogue Valley, from the round of 32 to the top

In 2090 Rogue Valley was 25-12 and out in the round of 32 for the fourth year in a row. Then came two waves of arrivals. The 2091 cohort took it to a league title and a semifinal. This year three more players came in: Bernard Celestine from Buckeye Ridge, Lake Iginla from Four Rivers Charter and Brandon Wininger from Three Saints. Rogue Valley went 36-2.

- Celestine went 24-4 at No. 2 singles and was the Del Rey Athletic Association player of the year.
- Iginla went 24-3 at No. 1 doubles and made the all state second team.
- Wininger went 20-2 at No. 3 doubles.

Rogue Valley beat Morgan Park 9-0 in the semifinal and Canal View 5-4 in the final. It then won three straight Tournament of Champions duals, two of them 3-2.

The window may be short. Rogue Valley brings back 30 percent of its flight wins, the lowest of any champion, because most of the 2091 cohort were juniors.

### The girls who moved up

Canal View girls went from 17-13 to 30-6 and reached the 9A semifinals, where Rockridge beat them 7-2. The four arrivals all played. Norma Stirling and Genesis Smith played singles, and Calliope Green and Zoey Machado played No. 1 doubles.

Rogue Valley girls went from 13-14 to 29-4, won the league and reached the 9A semifinals. Southridge Christian beat them 7-2 there.

- Arianna Noble went 21-6 at No. 1 singles and Hannah Francisco 28-3 at No. 2.
- Five of the seven arrivals made the all state first team: Noble, Isa Seymore, Trinity George, Elizabeth Malagon and Lilah Goshorn.

Kernwood County girls, 7-20 last year, went 26-5. They won their league and reached the 6A semifinals, where Perry Green beat them 5-2. Aline Cazorla went 23-8 at No. 2 singles. Tessa Werner and Addison Wirth made the all state second team.

Kernwood County boys went 28-3 and also reached the 6A semifinals, losing 5-2 to Cheney. Michele Girelli made the all state first team at No. 2 doubles.

### Cheney, Cedar and Kuusamo

Cheney boys won 6A without a one point dual in the bracket. They finished 30-1 and beat Central West End 7-0 in the final. Modibo Dick, 32-1, was the 6A player of the year.

Cedar won Group 1 by a point three times in five rounds, including 5-4 over Irvington in the semifinal and 5-4 over Monongahela in the final. Cedar is the program that handed Furnace Creek its only loss in 2089.

Kuusamo won Group 3 from the 11 seed at 19-9, the lowest seed and the worst record of any champion. It beat the top seed Ninemile 4-1 in the final. Ninemile has now lost the Group 3 final in both genders.

### Firsts and long waits

- **Boys:**
  - Rogue Valley, Kuusamo and Fogline reached a first state final in the records that begin in 2027.
  - Glenwood and Christ the King (1A) and Burnt River (Group 3) reached a first semifinal. Glenwood is one of the Columbia Gorge District schools.
  - Sotomayor reached a semifinal for the first time in 42 years, Kuopio in 37 and Fairgrounds in 35.
  - Hampton reached a final for the first time in 33 years and Four Rivers Charter in 27.
- **Girls:**
  - Foxtail Meadows and Doyleville Union reached a first semifinal.
  - Ninemile reached a final for the first time in 60 years, Blackpine in 34 and California Beach in 30.
  - Tunnelton won for the first time since 2064 and Grizzly Gulch for the first time since 2062.

The 2091 report named teams whose dual records had outrun or trailed their flight records, and said they would move back toward their flights.

Ransoms Landing fell from 21-9 to 9-19. Gottschalk-Herman girls went from 28-8 to 31-1 and won 2A. Across the full sets of 40 luckiest and 40 unluckiest teams, the pattern held for a third straight year. The luckiest gave back 7 to 12 points and the unluckiest gained 8 to 12.

The 2092 champion projections, which ranked the top three in each class, had the champion in 12 of 24 classes and 21 of the 48 finalists.

Returning production held up too. Among 2091 teams that won at least 70 percent of their duals, those bringing back 70 percent or more of their flight wins finished level in 2092. Those bringing back less than half fell about 12 points.

## Notes on the data

Programs are matched on program id throughout. Records, honors and titles are as the exports state them. No player ratings are used anywhere in this report. Brackets and finals are taken from the championships file.

Three girls sent in the intake transfers are not on any 2092 roster: Gabriela Schroeder (to Kernwood County), Morag Murray (to Sunset Hills) and Juliette Huffman (to Purcell). The export does not say where they went.

A collapse means winning at least 70 percent of duals one year and under 30 percent the next.
