# JHSAA 2035 Season Retrospective and Association Report

The uploaded research export is internally `2109`; this report treats it as canonical **2035** under the established retrofit.

## Canon note: the season stands as played

2035 was simulated before the latest private-school status and expansion changes were applied to the working checkout. **There is no retroactive reclassification, reseeding, replay, or title reassignment.** The map that actually produced the schedules and postseason is the official 2035 map.

The later rulings are treated as **2036 changes**: Evans Larsen Day moves from public 7A to private 10B; Basalt returns from 11B to public 4A; Peregrine returns from 11B to public 3A; Washington San Cordero returns from 11B to public 1A; the 25-school private expansion debuts; and Evans becomes Tamarack County. The 2035 archive retains the names and classes under which the season was played.

The largest consequence is visible rather than hypothetical: **Evans Larsen Day's girls won 7A. That remains an official 7A championship.**

## Executive findings

- **Mater Dei boys went 46-1, won 10B and won the Tournament of Champions**, beating Canyonlands 3-2 in the TOC final.
- **Gottschalk-Herman girls won the TOC as a 10B State runner-up for the second straight year.** After losing the State final 5-4 to Mater Dei, they won the qualifier and then beat four State champions in a row: Seawall, Evans Larsen Day, Romero-Finniski and Mater Dei.
- The fixed **15/16 qualifier seeds plus State-final rematch protection** worked exactly as designed. Gottschalk-Herman received the hard low-seed path but could not meet Mater Dei again until the final; it then won the rematch 4-1.
- **Romero-Finniski swept 11B**, going 34-1 boys and 35-2 girls. Condotti Vanguard Academy reached the 11B quarterfinals in both genders.
- The old two-team Sunkist anomaly is gone. CV and RF played in the full 11-member Cape-Meridian League; RF won both league races and CV finished second.
- **Circuit champions won two State titles**: Taft girls in 4A and Latgaway girls in Group 3. Bianchi boys also won a Circuit and reached the 4A State final.
- Zonal champions still won **16 of 18 titles in the nine Circuit classes**, down from 17 of 18 in each of the first two Circuit seasons.
- Three schools swept a class across genders: **Netherwood (3A), Mater Dei (10B), Romero-Finniski (11B)**.
- First State titles in the archive went to **Netherwood boys, Iron Gate girls and Lewis girls**.
- **Chaparral boys won a third straight 9A State title.**
- **Evans Larsen Day girls repeated in 7A**, going 37-3 and reaching the TOC quarterfinal in the final season before the school's move to the Non-Public road.
- No coaching carousel cycle was committed between 2034 and 2035: all 940 boys and 977 girls head-coach seats carried over unchanged.
- Grades 7-8 contained **662 boys and 721 girls**; 414 boys and 438 girls appeared in varsity play.
- The rising-freshman portal moved **158 boys and 203 girls**; 157 boys and all 203 girls appeared in varsity competition.
- There were **zero district doubleheaders** in either gender.

## Tournament of Champions

Boys qualifier winners were **Orchard Union** over Natchitoches 3-2 and **Walter-Kenny** over Banfield Day 4-1. Walter-Kenny then beat Kishwaukee 3-2 in round one. Mater Dei's TOC path was Orchard Union 3-2, Chaparral 3-2, Jackson Hole 4-1 and Canyonlands 3-2.

Girls qualifier winners were **Pacific Gate** over Sherwood Bench 4-1 and **Gottschalk-Herman** over Cassius 3-2. Both immediately beat State champions: Pacific Gate beat Canal View 3-2; Gottschalk-Herman beat Seawall 4-1.

Gottschalk-Herman then beat Evans Larsen Day 4-1, Romero-Finniski 3-2 and Mater Dei 4-1. The bracket therefore gave the qualifier no soft path while still preventing an immediate replay of the 10B final.

Gottschalk-Herman has now reached **four consecutive State finals without winning one**, but has won the last **two TOCs**. The finalist-qualifier has produced four TOC championship seasons in seven years: Pacific Gate girls, Jesuit boys and Gottschalk-Herman girls twice.

## 11B: Romero-Finniski's return

Romero-Finniski's recent arc is one of the sharpest reversals in the association.

Boys: 7-10 in 2031, 3-15 in 2032, 4-10 in 2033, 26-3 in 2034, then **34-1 and State champion** in 2035.

Girls: 3-10 in 2031, 7-11 in 2032, 15-6 in 2033, 30-3 in 2034, then **35-2 and State champion** in 2035.

The comeback happened in a normal league. Cape-Meridian held 11 programs. RF went 15-0 boys and 15-1 girls in district play; Condotti Vanguard went 13-2 and 14-1 and reached both State quarterfinals.

## The outgoing private-school map

The later status rulings are prospective and do not alter this archive.

- **Evans Larsen Day** played public 7A: boys 23-7 and a State Octofinalist; girls 37-3, State champion and TOC quarterfinalist. It enters 10B in 2036.
- **Basalt** played 11B: boys 25-11, Shasta League champion and State Octofinalist; girls 19-11, State Round of 24. It returns to 4A in 2036.
- **Peregrine** played 11B: boys 15-19, Conference exit; girls 20-11, State Octofinalist. It returns to 3A in 2036.
- **Washington San Cordero** played 11B and missed State in both genders. It returns to 1A in 2036.
- The 25 new private programs debut in 2036.

The official 2035 Non-Public populations therefore remain 54 boys / 57 girls in 10B and 58 boys / 59 girls in 11B. The league structure itself was healthy: each class had six ordinary leagues and no two-team Sunkist remnant.

## Continuity, firsts and long waits

Repeat champions from 2034 were Chaparral, Miles Davis and Jackson Hole among the boys; Seawall, Evans Larsen Day and Canal View among the girls.

Netherwood, Mater Dei and Romero-Finniski swept their classes across genders.

First State titles went to Netherwood boys, Iron Gate girls and Lewis girls. Fort Meriwether boys ended a **56-season** title gap; Brackwood Pass girls a **69-season** gap; Latgaway girls a **40-season** gap. Romero-Finniski's droughts had reached twelve boys seasons and thirteen girls seasons.

Across all State tournaments, lower seeds won 22.9% of boys duals and 21.5% of girls duals. Only five champions came from outside the top three seeds: Bush and Doyleville Union boys; Taft, Netherwood and Latgaway girls. Thirteen of the 28 finals were decided by one point or the Group 2 tiebreak procedure.

## Coaching

No carousel cycle was committed between 2034 and 2035. There were **zero head-coach changes, zero assistant-to-head promotions and zero staff moves** in either gender. The season was played under the same coaching assignments as 2034.

## Association judgment for 2036

2035 is an official season played under an outgoing membership map, not a season to reconstruct. The later status rulings and private expansion alter **2036**.

Competitively, the Circuit had its strongest validation yet and the TOC qualifier rule received an unusually clean proof case. Gottschalk-Herman was forced onto the low seed line, protected from an immediate State-final replay, beat three other State champions, then earned the Mater Dei rematch and won it.

The main structural change entering 2036 is therefore not another playoff redesign. It is the membership map: 25 new private programs, Evans Larsen Day into 10B, and Basalt, Peregrine and Washington San Cordero back onto the public ladder.
