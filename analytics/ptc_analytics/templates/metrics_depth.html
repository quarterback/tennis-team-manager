Depth & Volatility

Top-End = .6×S1% + .4×D1%. Depth = mean win% across S3+/D2+. Star Dependence = Top-End − Depth — big positive means one or two players are carrying the program. Floor/Ceiling are the 25th/75th percentile of per-dual line share; Volatility is the spread between duals. Blowout rate = wins by 80%+ of that dual's lines; Resistance rate = losses where the team still took 40%+ of the lines.
