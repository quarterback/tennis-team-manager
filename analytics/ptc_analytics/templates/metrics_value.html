Player Value (PVAR)

Crude Player Value Above Replacement: for every slot a player logged 3+ matches at, replacement level is the 25th-percentile win rate among other players at that same slot in that same season. PVAR = (their win rate − replacement) × matches played, summed across every slot and season on record. Deliberately not split by singles/doubles — the point is a number that looks past S%/D% at what a player was worth wherever the coach used them. No opponent adjustment yet; a first cut, not a finished model.
