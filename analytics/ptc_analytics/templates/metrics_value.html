Player Value

Two numbers that measure the same career from opposite ends, and they are meant to disagree. PVAR asks whether the seat was filled better than the next player would have filled it: replacement level is the 25th-percentile win rate among other players at that same flight in that same season, and PVAR is (their rate − replacement) × matches, summed. It never looks at ability. WAE asks whether they beat the matches they were actually handed: every flight is priced on the OVR gap it was contested at, and WAE is wins minus that price. A player can lead one and not the other — a strong player winning the matches their ability says they should wins PVAR and sits near zero on WAE, which is the correct reading of both.
