Predictive

A crude, auto-scaled power-based win-probability model (logistic on TOSS power, scaled to the season's own spread — only comparable within one season/gender) drives everything here. Record Luck = actual dual wins − model-expected wins; positive means the record is stronger than the model expects. Upset Value rewards wins as a clear underdog (favors small samples less than a flat "upset count"); Bad-Loss Value mirrors it for losses as a favorite. Elite Win Share = wins vs top-decile-power opponents ÷ all wins. Needs opponent power on file for both sides of a dual — thin without it.
