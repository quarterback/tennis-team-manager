Format Lift

RCI = expected court share under the regular-season card. SCI = expected court share under the state/postseason card. Both card shapes are read off the actual duals in each season — never assumed — so they track a season correctly even after the game changes its own dual formats. Fmt = SCI − RCI, in percentage points — how much the postseason format shape helps or hurts a team. State Win% is the modeled chance of winning a neutral postseason-shaped dual (needs a majority of that card's lines), assuming independent courts. A "—" means this season has no duals on record in that phase/round yet, not that the format is unsupported.
