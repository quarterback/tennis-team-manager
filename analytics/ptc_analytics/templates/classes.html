The design's claim is that a bigger school does not simply have better players: the top ends sit close together and enrollment buys DEPTH, so the gap between classes should be small at position 1 and widen going down. Read the columns left to right — if the classes fan out as you go, the shape is holding; if they are already far apart at position 1, it is not. This is a live world, so this measures where talent ENDED UP after every transfer, which is a different question from what the generator promises.
